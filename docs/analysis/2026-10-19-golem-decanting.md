## Root-owned permission and transfer boundary

- The FINAL fishing-api agreement exposes **`GolemHarvestPlatform.mayInteract(worker,pos)`** for loaded/world/border/profile/spawn checks. - The actor must be **non-OP**, correctly attributed to the worker/owner under root policy; never borrow an online player's ServerPlayer, connection, permissions or OP status. Native source-fluid removal must fire real **BEFORE and AFTER** removal/break callbacks with the **same captured real old water source BlockState** (BE=null). Run preflight/BEFORE on the real old state, then **re-read actual world state after every callback** plus worker/cargo/target identity. Never force-load chunks.
- **Commit:** root coordinates native source removal and **exactly +1000 actual cargo** under a small same-thread/reentrancy guard, securing capacity before mutation. No source change before permission/capacity, no credit before confirmed removal of that captured source. Source removal may itself run callbacks: check the actual empty/replaced state and ownership again on return, not just a stale success boolean. The coordinator must distinguish its paid removal from unrelated mutation, not blindly refund or re-collect a changed cell. Voluntary recall/core edits cannot split this operation.
- **After:** publish the confirmed carried ownership **before AFTER/feedback callbacks** can recall/remove the worker; AFTER receives the same real **old source** state, not current air. Re-read actual empty/replaced state and worker/cargo after each callback; a new source/replacement is not another entitlement in this pulse, and post-commit changes must not erase paid cargo. No saved source-position retry in another world, no restoration over a replacement, no journal/escrow/provider layer.
- **Sink:** independently permission-check the actual loaded home crucible and re-resolve the exact BE after each callback. Root's guarded debit/fill/remainder operation must prevent recall/reentrant delivery from seeing both spendable old cargo and filled water: debit the attempted transfer before effectful fill/sync, restore only demonstrably unaccepted mB, retain the rest.
