Never interpret raw `canAttackPlayers()==true` as ordinary PvP permission.

Separately, the target's **floored block position must have home-distance²<256**, not simply actor-distance≤16 (`AINearestAttackableTarget:46–64`, sorter:24–27; E:460–473,1157–1159). ## Minimal playable native unit / safety boundary

- Extend finite supported core **GUARD4**, actual Core item, root **CORE_GUARD / coreForType(4)**, recipe/book alias/tooltip/icon (`golem_core_guard`), entity install/recall and GUI dispatch. Reuse existing material/home/name configuration; six ghost carrier fields stay empty/zero and are not Guard controls. No new target-marker schema, player weapon stock or input subsystem is needed.
- Proposed bounded runtime methods: **`isGuardTarget(LivingEntity)`** shared by acquisition, retaliation, chase and strike; **`getAttackSpeed()`=20**, guarded mob-damage execution, and existing action/status4. Revalidate live/same-level target, home range, category, allies/tame/protected-passive exclusions and entity-harm permission **at each strike**, not merely acquisition. Keep the native hurt/cancellation pipeline; no raw health subtraction or OP/fake-player damage attribution. Block/crop harvest permission is not entity-combat authorization.
- **Defer player/PvP and animal/creeper category enablement with Order.** Never let a generic HurtByTargetGoal turn retaliation into player/pet attacks. Future PvP requires explicit server/owner identity, PvP setting, team/friendly-fire, creative/spectator/invulnerability and claim/entity-harm checks; mob damage alone does not enforce player-versus-player rules. Original nearest acquisition bypasses the parent suitability/sight helper, and its strike lacks an explicit visibility test: requiring native sight/no through-wall hits is a disclosed safety correction, not quoted source behavior.
- This makes ordinary melee Guard useful without upgrades; it does not claim upgraded targeting, full combat visuals or all golems. **Lumber remains separately deferred until a real Axe of the Stream acquisition/item exists**—never a fake infusion ingredient, and not a blocker for Guard progression.
