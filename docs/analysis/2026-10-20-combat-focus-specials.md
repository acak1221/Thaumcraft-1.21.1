# Fire, Frost and Shock focus specializations

Gameplay authority is the private original TC4 4.2.3.5 oracle: `ItemFocusFire`, `ItemFocusFrost`, `ItemFocusShock`, `FocusUpgradeType`, `EntityEmber`, `EntityFrostShard`, `EntityExplosiveOrb`, `EntityUtils.getEntitiesInRange`, `PacketFXZap`, and their original renderers. The retained 1.12 sources are not the specification. Earthshock's separate projectile and airy10 field are recorded by their author independently.

## Original contracts implemented

The closed native catalogue adds original IDs4 alchemist's fire (Energy+Slime),5 alchemist's frost (Cold+Trap),9 fireball (Darkness),10 firebeam (Energy+Air),11 scattershot (Cold+Weapon),12 ice boulder (Cold+Crystal),17 chain lightning and18 earthshock (Weather each). Other unknown IDs remain rejected. Each aspect costs one; catalogue returns are defensive. Existing Frugal/Potency/Treasure/Enlarge/Architect/Extend/Silk8/Dowsing20 retain their identities.

Actual casts call `getVisCost` with the **outer wand** in all three original focus classes. Their specialization tests therefore see no upgrades and pay the base price, including creative: Fire10 Ignis; Frost5 Aqua+2 Ignis+2 Perditio; Shock25 Aer. Each primal is multiplied by the original float modifier then truncated. The actual focus tooltip separately advertises Firebeam10 Ignis+3 Ordo, Fireball66 Ignis+33 Perditio, Scatter20 Aqua+2 Ignis+2 Perditio+5 Aer, Boulder20 Aqua+2 Ignis+2 Perditio+5 Terra, Chain40 Aer+10 Aqua and Earthshock75 Aer+25 Terra. This mismatch is preserved deliberately.

Fire offers alchemist at ranks2/4, fireball or beam at3, and Frugal/Potency at every rank. A fireball focus with an existing alchemist rank refuses another alchemist upgrade. Fireball uses WAVE,1000ms cooldown, but retains the original per-tick tooltip label; it performs one paid spawn attempt with strength1+0.4P and the presence of alchemist enables explosion fire. A beam remains a per-tick channel and emits2+P embers with scatter0.25, damage(2+P+0.5)*1.5 and duration30. Ordinary embers retain scatter15, damage2+P and duration20. Alchemist levels extend ignition from3 to3+level seconds and give the original0.025*level block-impact chance to place ordinary fire in adjacent air. Ember terrain writes retain their existing original raw-placement policy.

Frost offers alchemist at1/3/5 and scatter or boulder at3. Scatter emits5+2P distinct damage1 fragile shards, scatter8, gravity0.015. Boulder emits one damage4+2P shard with bounce0.8 and six remaining bounces; the seventh actual impact terminates it. Ordinary shards remain damage3+1.5P, bounce0.5, four terminal impacts and gravity0.05. Scatter/Boulder cooldown is500ms; ordinary Frost is200ms. Alchemist applies200ticks Slowness with amplifier level−1. Original fragile collisions reset the victim's immunity timer after hurt and reduce the change in velocity to one tenth. The original applies slowing/fragile effects even when damage was refused; this is preserved. No water-freezing or ice-placement effect is invented.

Shock offers Chain/Earthshock at3 and Enlarge at4/5 only with one of those specializations. Raw native component data cannot bypass that prerequisite. Chain keeps CHARGE with500ms cooldown, pays before the initial entity ray and gives the primary6+P damage, then2+2Enlarge secondary jumps at4+P. Each jump selects the nearest unvisited living entity from the current center's8-block **AABB**, excluding the caster and players when server PvP is disabled. It is not a spherical search and does not add a line-of-sight check. Refused damage can still be a chain center. Source/target body midpoints use the existing server-only visual route. Earthshock uses WAVE/1000ms and one orb with area4+2Enlarge and damage integer-truncated5+1.33P.

## Projectile and authority adaptations

The new explosive orb uses the original throwable muzzle, one three-Gaussian scatter1 launch at1.5 speed, gravity0.01, native inherited flight and four-tick thrower grace. Direct damage is strength*1.5 and retains the thrower. Original direct damage has both fire and magic flags: two native damage types preserve fire/projectile tags and witch resistance without armour bypass, including the ownerless original `onFire` fallback. The explosion itself has no source owner, strength as above, optional fire and `ExplosionInteraction.NONE`, so it damages entities without destroying blocks. Native exposure, protections, fluid/portal physics and fire placement replace the old vanilla engine. Hurt can redirect motion to an attacker's look*0.9 and retains its old thrower. It expires after500 air ticks. Paid strength/fire/lifetime and Frost's advanced state/earned impacts are saved and synchronized; the original omitted some of this state and could rejuvenate after reload.

Payment commits to the actual hand before native spawn/damage callbacks. Cancelled spawn attempts stay paid, with no refund/retry. Reentrant focus dispatch is already guarded. Replacing a hand/dimension/use channel in a callback stops any remaining paid stream, scatter or chain rather than lending it to a replacement. Native damage protections still own each hurt; the explicit original Frost post-hurt effects remain as described above.

Original explosive/electric atlas billboards, animated fireball trails and frosty sparkles have native client implementations. The existing low-alpha particle shader/render-state helper supplies their client rendering; no server class loads a client class. Client rendering remains uninspected.
