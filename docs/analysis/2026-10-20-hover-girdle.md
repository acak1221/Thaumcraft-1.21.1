The belt callback does not duplicate those effects. Traveller Boots' separate `0.25F` fall adjustment also remains in the shared movement phase, after the existing accessory loop.

The focused native runtime scenario uses a real connected survival player, the actual worn BELT slot and native connection/player living ticks. Its fall history is a declared boundary input on a stationary suspended player, with a mundane-belt control.
