# Thaumatorium — original contract and Fabric implementation, 20.10.2026

Specification: original TC4 4.2.3.5 private oracle, outside this checkout: `WandManager.createThaumatorium`, `TileThaumatorium`, `TileThaumatoriumTop`, `ContainerThaumatorium`, `GuiThaumatorium`, `BlockMetalDevice`, `InventoryUtils.placeItemStackIntoInventory`, `ConfigRecipes:97,294`, `ConfigResearch` (`THAUMATORIUM`). The retained 1.12 source is not the gameplay authority.

## Baseline behavior

- A crucible and two alchemical constructs form a vertical column. Clicking either construct with a wand requires THAUMATORIUM and spends **15 Fire, 30 Order, 30 Water whole vis**, priced through the real wand/cap/gear discounts. The two constructs become the lower machine and its upper proxy; the existing crucible remains the same block entity. Output facing is the clicked side, including the original unusual vertical facings.
- One real catalyst slot, accessible to automation from every side, is shared by both halves. The ordinary machine remembers **one** chosen formula. A correctly oriented **Mnemonic Matrix** adds **two** formulas; a matrix may connect to either half, excluding down and the output face. The ordinary horizontal machine has seven usable positions (capacity 15); the original vertical-facing quirks allow capacities 17/19. Attachments refresh on real placement/removal/neighbor events and every 40 ticks. Removing matrices truncates selected formulas from the end without deleting already paid essence. The matrix recipe is `IAI/ABA/IAI`: four iron ingots, four amber and one zombie brain; Fire/Water/Order 5 vis each.
- The menu offers researched crucible recipes whose catalyst matches, plus any formula already selected. Selecting a formula records its author. The previous active formula is reused while its catalyst still matches; otherwise formulas are scanned in their installed order. Recipe references use native recipe IDs rather than unstable legacy integer hashes; old worlds are outside the authorized scope.
- Check heat at the machine's `y−2` on the first tick and every 40 ticks: fire, lava or nitor. Work every **5 ticks** while heated and without redstone at the crucible, lower machine or upper machine. The next requested aspect is the first insufficient one in lexical aspect-tag order. Suction is **128** while an aspect is requested. Pull one unit per work step through either machine half, excluding the output face and down, and excluding the lower half's up face.
- A container at the output must have room for the **whole** output before any additional essence is pulled or a catalyst is spent. Completion consumes **one** catalyst and clears the complete accumulated aspect list (including stale aspects left by changing formulas, an original quirk). An online formula author receives the original item-crafting warp callback once after the paid craft; output quantity does not multiply warp.
- Output enters a known vanilla chest, hopper or furnace using prepared native inventory field replacements. Double-chest order preserves the original oddity: merge near, merge far, try all near slots, merge far again, with no insertion into an empty far-half slot. Other mod containers remain unverified and cause a pause rather than an unsafe debit.
- Without an inventory in front, a paid result enters the existing durable item-drop queue at `x+.5+dx*.66, y+.33−dy, z+.5+dz*.66` with velocity `(.075dx,.025,.075dz)`. A paid physical result also persists in the tile until queue publication. Destruction evacuates the one actual catalyst inventory once; each formed half drops its original alchemical construct.

## Preservation and deviations

Both resource owners are prepared and checked before callback-free field replacement. Public output plans cannot spend a catalyst without the exact live machine completion scope. The upper proxy resolves the current lower tile for every access; unloaded or replaced halves are never cached as transfer authority. Unknown or malformed tile data is retained in a rejected payload and the machine remains closed. Invalid negative amounts do not reproduce the original duplication bugs, and insertion before a recipe exists safely refuses instead of reproducing the original null dereference.

Native input/output stacks are preserved, including data components. Recipe installation is checked again by the server menu; stale menus, invalid buttons and unresearched formulas cannot install a job. The machine continues an already installed formula when its author goes offline, as the original did. Formation rechecks the actual held wand and both world blocks after pricing before spending anything.
